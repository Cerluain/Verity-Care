Real case · Arizona

Hacienda HealthCare · Phoenix

"Intentional inflating of costs" billed to AHCCCS, 2013–2018.

Agreed to repay ~$10.9M plus a $1M fine. The CEO pleaded guilty in 2021.

Hacienda is an intermediate care facility for people with intellectual disabilities, not a nursing home. It's the same cost-inflation pattern, in Arizona long-term care.

[AZ5][AZ6] AZ Attorney General via Arizona Daily Independent (Sep 2021) & 12News (Jun 2021). source · source
